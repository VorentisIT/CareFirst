## About

Dr. Amara Okoro is a distinguished dermatologist and skin oncologist with 11 years of dedicated clinical practice. Her patient-centered consultations blend evidence-based diagnostic rigor with compassionate care for complex skin conditions.

## Philosophy

She believes skin health is an essential mirror of overall systemic wellbeing. By educating patients and providing modern therapies, she helps patients achieve enduring dermatological health and confidence.
