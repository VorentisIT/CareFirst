## About

Dr. David Miller is an experienced interventional cardiologist dedicated to saving lives through rapid, state-of-the-art cardiovascular catheterization and coronary interventions. With 14 years of cardiac expertise, he leads key clinical procedures at CareFirst Clinic.

## Philosophy

He is passionate about combining prompt acute intervention with sustainable lifestyle counseling, ensuring heart patients not only recover from acute events but thrive with renewed vitality.
