## About

Dr. Michael Chen is a recognized authority in clinical neurology with 18 years of clinical leadership. He serves as Chief of Neurology at CareFirst Clinic, guiding patients through complex brain, spine, and nerve disorders with clinical rigor and genuine empathy.

## Philosophy

He believes neurology requires both exacting diagnostic investigation and deep compassionate listening. Every neurological symptom tells an essential story, and patient recovery begins with accurate diagnosis and collaborative long-term care.

## Achievements

Author of more than 20 peer-reviewed neurological research papers and recipient of clinical excellence citations for innovative neuro-monitoring protocols.
